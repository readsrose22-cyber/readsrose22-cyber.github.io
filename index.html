🚀 About This Project

This is a fan-made, standalone VR adaptation of the legendary Metal Gear Solid series, built exclusively for Meta Quest headsets. Unlike other VR conversion projects, this one runs entirely on your headset — you don't need a powerful gaming computer or cables. Just install it on your Quest and dive into the stealth action wherever you are.

The project leverages OpenXR technology to provide smooth, optimized performance on your standalone device. It's designed for casual players and hardcore stealth fans alike — pick up and play, no complicated setup steps required.

📥 Download & Installation Guide

Getting started is incredibly simple. Just follow these 3 steps:

  • Step 1: Visit this link to download the application the official releases page.
  • Step 2: Download the files. You'll likely get a .zip file — download and extract this file, then run the application on your computer.
  • Step 3: Transfer the extracted app to your Quest headset using the built-in developer mode or a USB cable, then install and launch it.
🫂 Get the Game Here

Once installed, you can disconnect from your PC and play anywhere — your Quest is all you need.

🛠️ Setup Steps for Beginners

  • Make sure your Quest has Developer Mode activated (help is available on the Meta website).
  • Connect your headset to your computer via USB and allow file access when prompted.
  • Copy the extracted game folder (usually named MGS-VR) into the /Downloads/ folder on your Quest.
  • On your Quest, go to Library → Unknown Sources to see the game.
  • Launch it from there and enjoy the action!
